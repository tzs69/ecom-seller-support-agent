# Delivered-but-missing procedure: applicability

Fictional demo business policy; not a real platform policy.

## SCOPE-02: Delivered-but-missing only

This procedure applies to orders marked delivered for which the buyer disputes
receipt. Orders in transit or in another status require a different support
procedure and are not eligible for a carrier investigation request through this
procedure.

An order marked delivered with contradictory carrier tracking requires the
evidence-gap handling specified by the application, rather than assuming either
status is correct.
