# Delivery evidence and permitted resolutions

Fictional demo business policy; not a real platform policy. These resolution
rules apply only to eligible delivered-but-missing orders without a prior
resolution that blocks a new request under HISTORY-01.

## EVIDENCE-01: Missing delivery proof

Where there is no conflicting delivery location and both a delivery photo and
recipient signature are absent, the permitted next step is a carrier
investigation request, subject to seller approval before submission.

A delivered scan alone does not establish buyer receipt. A matching delivery
location alone does not replace photo or signature availability. This outcome
does not establish refund eligibility or a guaranteed investigation deadline.

## EVIDENCE-02: Conflicting delivery location

A recorded delivery location that differs from the order destination requires
human review of the discrepancy. This takes precedence over missing delivery
proof, even when both conditions are present. No automatic carrier investigation
request is permitted for this outcome.

An unknown delivery location is not a confirmed mismatch. A mismatch alone does
not establish fault or refund eligibility.

## EVIDENCE-03: Present, consistent proof with disputed receipt

Where a delivery photo reference or recipient signature is available and there
is no recorded location conflict, disputed receipt requires human review. This
outcome does not permit automatic carrier investigation request submission or
automatic rejection of the buyer's claim.

Proof availability alone does not establish its authenticity or that the buyer
personally received the parcel.
